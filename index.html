Common questions

Before we talk.

A few practical answers for brand owners considering a fulfilment partner.

What kinds of brands does BPFS work with?

BPFS is positioned for Canadian direct-to-consumer brands, with a particular focus on supplement and nutrition products.

What fulfilment services are available?

Core services include warehousing and storage, inbound receiving, pick and pack, daily order workflow, and basic inventory visibility.

How does shipping work?

Shipping and postage typically run through the client’s own carrier accounts. We can discuss how that fits your current setup during the quote process.

Is BPFS a large national 3PL?

BPFS takes a boutique approach. The focus is dedicated capacity, direct communication, and a closer working relationship—not being one account among thousands.

What information helps with a quote?

Your product type, sales channels, monthly order range, inventory needs, and any current fulfilment challenges are the best place to start.
